How it learns

1 · Working togetherDesign reviews and weekly sessions - knowledge passed person to person.
2 · Capture the whyA decision plus its reason, at the moment it's made - one tap, or words already said.
3 · Join it upReasons grouped by client, category and unit into studio rules; taste learnt from what's picked.
4 · Give it backThe rules go into the next brief's ideas - cited - and onto this page for new starters.

Based on Nonaka & Takeuchi's knowledge-creation spiral (SECI) and Polanyi's "we know more than we can tell". Nothing here costs AI credit - it runs on this PC.
